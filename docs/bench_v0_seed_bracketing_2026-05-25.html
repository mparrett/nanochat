The convergence wasn't

Stage 2 SC sat=100 was a lucky seed. Bracketing collapses the headline gap to ~5%, inside both arms' variance.

nanochat · experiment/hope-nested-learning · 2026-05-25

Two weeks ago bench v0 reported Stage 2 saturating on hard SelectiveCopy at step 100, vs baseline at step 200 — a clean 2× architectural win and the centerpiece of the project map's "convergence" framing. Today's seed bracketing falsifies it. With n=3 on Stage 2 and n=4 on baseline, the mean gap is 11 steps (~5%) and Stage 2's variance is wider than baseline's. The s0=100 reading was a lucky seed. The architectural difference is invisible against the seed-noise floor at this scale.

What this falsifies

From docs/bench_v0_stage2_sc_hard_2026-05-19.md:

Stage 2 saturates 2× faster than baseline and 3.75× faster than Stage 1. Final accuracy higher than both. This is a clean architectural win on the state-tracking axis at d4.
  • "2× faster than baseline" → ✗ falsified. Mean ratio 219/208 = 1.05.
  • "3.75× faster than Stage 1" → Stage 1 wasn't bracketed today. Best-case still-defensible after Stage 1 bracketing pending.
  • "Final accuracy higher than both" → ~1pp gap (0.999 vs 0.988). Real but not load-bearing.
  • "A clean architectural win on the state-tracking axis at d4" → ✗ falsified.

The writeup itself anticipated this exact possibility, to its credit:

n=1 seed. Need at least n=2-3 before declaring "Stage 2 wins on state-tracking" as more than a lucky-seed result. Magnitude (100 vs 200) makes pure-luck hard to swallow but doesn't rule it out.

Pure-luck turned out to be the explanation.

Methodology lesson

n=1 is insufficient for any architectural claim on bench/. Seed variance at d4/500-iter scale is large enough to swamp the architectural differences bench v0 reported as "wins."

Today's session is the second instance of n=1 → n=N collapse this morning. NTK-Mirror Path A earlier today reported +1/30 over LoRA on persona-retention; bracketing collapsed it to parity. The same lesson now applies one tier down at our own bench v0 results.

Proposed bench/ default going forward: every architectural comparison runs n=3 minimum before any "X beats Y" claim. An --n-seeds N flag on bench/run.py would make this trivial (10-15 line change, internal loop, report mean ± range). Worth doing if any further bench/ work happens.

What does NOT change

  • The d6/5000-iter LM verdict ("Stage 2 neutral on natural-language val_bpb under recipe-controlled comparison"). That had its own multi-seed validation in docs/hope_nl_stage2_seed_variance_2026-05-04.md.
  • The δ-mem field result on hard MQAR at 100× scale. Independent evidence on different scale and different mechanism; stands on its own.
  • The architectural-shape hypothesis itself ("memory mechanisms are task-mechanism-routing problems, not 'memory yes/no'"). The hypothesis is still live; the d4 bench v0 evidence for it just isn't.
